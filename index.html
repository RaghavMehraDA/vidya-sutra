Our campus belief

Make the handover safer

Before you pass on a device, protect people and data.

Responsible donation starts before collection. Follow device-maker instructions and local guidance where they differ.

YOUR PERSONAL DATA

Give your information a clean break.

Back up anything you need, sign out of accounts, remove SIM and memory cards, and use the manufacturer’s guidance to erase or reset the device. Never share passwords or unlock codes.

DEVICE CONDITION

Keep reusable items together.

Note whether an item works, needs repair or no longer powers on. Avoid mixing electronics with household waste or leaving them where they can be damaged.

BATTERY SAFETY

Keep batteries out of general waste.

Do not crush, puncture, charge or dismantle batteries at home. Keep loose batteries separate and follow local authorized collection advice.

Swollen, hot or leaking battery?Do not handle or transport it yourself. Keep it away from heat and seek local authorized safety guidance.
AUTHORIZED ROUTES

Ask who handles the materials.

Before any real handover, check accepted items, partner credentials, safe storage and whether you’ll receive a clear record of the collection.

Partner details will be shared only once verified.

If you’re unsure, don’t dismantle or place the item in a public bin—ask your local waste authority or an authorized recycler for safe guidance.
